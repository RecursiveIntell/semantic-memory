     1	# TurboQuant Readiness
     2	
     3	TurboQuant support in `semantic-memory` is feature-gated behind
     4	`turbo-quant-codec`. Raw SQLite f32 embeddings remain authoritative; TurboQuant
     5	artifacts are derived acceleration/transport artifacts only.
     6	
     7	## Current Status
     8	
     9	- TurboQuant is not the default retrieval backend.
    10	- Live TurboQuant candidate generation is available only when
    11	  `turbo-quant-codec` is enabled and
    12	  `SearchConfig::derived_vector_backend` is set to
    13	  `turbo_quant_candidate_only`.
    14	- Exact f32 rerank remains enabled by default for TurboQuant candidates.
    15	- The codec path uses BLAKE3 `stack-ids::ContentDigest` profile and artifact
    16	  identities rendered as `blake3:<64-hex>`.
    17	- TurboQuant artifacts use deterministic `TQW1` binary wire bytes at the codec
    18	  boundary.
    19	- Artifact profile and encoded-byte digests are checked before decode/score.
    20	- Malformed Polar/QJL/Turbo codes fail closed.
    21	- Prepared query scoring is available in `turbo-quant`.
    22	- Derived artifacts are stored in `derived_vector_artifacts` and can be rebuilt
    23	  from authoritative SQLite f32 embeddings with
    24	  `MemoryStore::rebuild_vector_artifacts()`.
    25	- Live TurboQuant receipts include vector artifact manifest digest, artifact
    26	  counts, missing/stale counts, approximate candidate count, exact rerank count,
    27	  and fallback reason when applicable.
    28	- Criterion benchmarks are available in `turbo-quant` for encode throughput,
    29	  scoring throughput, and candidate generation.
    30	
    31	## Runtime Limits
    32	
    33	TurboQuant remains an optional candidate backend, not a source of truth. The
    34	runtime falls back to brute-force raw f32 search when artifacts are missing,
    35	incomplete, stale, corrupt, or when SQL filters are active. Receipts record the
    36	candidate backend, codec family/profile digest, artifact counts, fallback reason,
    37	and degradation notes.
    38	
    39	The P31 deterministic benchmark gate classifies the current standard local
    40	profile as green for feature-gated candidate generation. This does not make
    41	TurboQuant a default backend.
    42

     7	## Summary
     8

    34	- Validated encoded artifact digest before decode/score.
    35	- Switched `TurboQuantCodec` from JSON artifacts to binary TurboQuant wire bytes.

    41	- Added backwards-compatible search context and receipt hook fields for trace, attempt family, attempt, replay, query/filter digests, redaction state, budget/deadline, artifact manifest digest, artifact counters, approximate candidate count, exact rerank count, and fallback reason.
